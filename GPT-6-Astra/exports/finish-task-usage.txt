Methodology:
- Tokens come from the final event_msg/token_count.info.total_token_usage in each local session. Repeated cumulative counters are not added again.
- Per-turn tokens are cumulative-counter differences. Their sums were checked against each session total; each distinct delta also matched last_token_usage.
- Main-chat totals exclude subagents. Combined totals sum the main chat and its three identified child sessions, including their follow-up turns.
- Cached input is included in input_tokens; reasoning output is included in output_tokens. total_tokens = input_tokens + output_tokens. Do not add the subsets again.
- Token counts include repeatedly submitted conversation context. They are recorded model usage, not unique text size or a monetary bill.
- Elapsed task time is the main turn duration_ms. Summed agent turn durations overlap because agents worked concurrently; they are not additional elapsed time or model-only inference time.
- Turn timestamps use log event timestamps, displayed in UTC and Asia/Shanghai. Reported duration_ms is retained because subtracting log timestamps can differ slightly due to event recording.
- Inherited parent task_started entries in child logs have no matching child completion and are excluded from child turn durations.
- This export operation is excluded. Conversation text, tool output, and account-level rate-limit and credit data are not exported.
